# STRAND — Minimalist Grooming App & Salon Intelligence

> **Where your haircut directives are strictly understood. 100% organic reviews & reference photo matching.**

---

## 🌟 Key Updates

### 1. Minimalist Warm Beige Aesthetic
- **Palette**: Soft linen oatmeal beige (`#F7F4EF`), porcelain ivory surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), and deep espresso charcoal typography (`#211F1C`).
- **De-cluttered Interface**: Generous whitespace, clean horizontal cards, and a peaceful CartoDB light map.

### 2. Hair Strand Logo with Subtle S-Curve
- A natural, delicate single hair strand with a subtle, organic S-wave curve (not a rigid typographical S), complete with a follicle root dot.

### 3. Haircut Reference Photo Upload
- **Upload Any Photo**: Add a reference photo directly from your phone camera or computer.
- **Inspiration Samples**: Pick from popular reference cuts (Low Fade, Textured Crop, Curly Shape, Wolf Cut, Classic Taper).
- **Displayed to the Barber**:
  - Visible on the homepage Directive Banner.
  - Showcased prominently on the printable / digital **Strand Directive Spec Card**.
  - Attached directly to appointment bookings.

### 4. Installable Progressive Web App (PWA)
- Complete with `manifest.json`, Service Worker `sw.js` for offline caching, and native install prompt support.
- Tap **Install App** in the navigation or "Add to Home Screen" on your mobile browser.
